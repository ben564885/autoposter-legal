Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026.

Autoposter is used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's YouTube channel (upload videos, read public comments on those videos) and the owner's Instagram account (publish Reels, read comments on those Reels).

From comments it reads only the text, to count one-word votes. It stores vote totals, not commenter names. It does not sell or share any data, and it does not use data for advertising.

Access tokens stay on the owner's private server. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and in Instagram settings.
